# The Medallion - Watch Assignments

This document outlines the standard watch assignments for the crew of "The Medallion".

## The Seven Watches of the Day
A typical watch system on a sailing ship divided the 24-hour day into shifts. The period from 4 p.m. to 8 p.m. was divided into two shorter, two-hour "dog watches" to ensure the watch teams rotated their duty times each day.

| Watch Name      | Time Period         | Description                                        |
|:----------------|:--------------------|:---------------------------------------------------|
| Middle Watch    | Midnight – 4:00 AM  | The "Graveyard Watch." Extremely cold and dark.    |
| Morning Watch   | 4:00 AM – 8:00 AM   | Included the sunrise and the "washing of the decks." |
| Forenoon Watch  | 8:00 AM – Noon      | The main period for heavy maintenance and drills.  |
| Afternoon Watch | Noon – 4:00 PM      | Often the hottest part of the day; drills continued. |
| First Dog Watch | 4:00 PM – 6:00 PM   | A short, 2-hour watch.                             |
| Last Dog Watch  | 6:00 PM – 8:00 PM   | The second 2-hour "half" watch.                    |
| First Watch     | 8:00 PM – Midnight  | The first watch of the "night cycle."              |
